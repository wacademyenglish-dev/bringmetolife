Track 12 • Grammar

Imperatives

We use Imperatives to give Commands, Warnings, or ask for Help.

⚠️

Golden Rule:

In English, a command has NO SUBJECT (No "You", "I", "He").

You wake me up.

Wake me up!

Track 16 • Grammar

Me vs My

In the chorus, she uses both. What is the difference?

ME (Object)

Receives the action.

"Save me."

MY (Possession)

Followed by a noun (thing).

"Call my name."

Track 17 • Practice

"Look at _______ eyes!"
> Correct! "Eyes" is a noun, so we use MY.
